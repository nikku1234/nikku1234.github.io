# Machined Quiet

A restrained product-editorial theme for a robotics systems portfolio. The visual language is calm, precise, and materially rich: the page should feel like an industrial product launch while the interactive simulation lab carries the technical depth.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#f5f5f7`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Primary ink: `#1d1d1f`
- Secondary ink: `#6e6e73`
- Hairline: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.10)`
- Focus blue: `#1473e6`
- Deep section: `#000000`
- Dark surface: `#101010`
- Dark secondary: `#a1a1a6`

## Typography

- Display and body: native system sans stack for clean, confident product typography.
- Technical labels only: DM Mono, used sparingly.
- Headlines use tight tracking, high contrast, and short lines.

## Shape and depth

- Large surfaces use 24–36 px radii.
- Controls use pill geometry.
- Depth comes from soft tonal separation and restrained shadows, never hard offset borders.

## Motion

- Slow, subtle product float in the hero.
- Short opacity/translate transitions for navigation and controls.
- Simulation animations remain expressive inside their dedicated dark environment.
- All non-essential motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Content rhythm

- Hero: one statement, one product object, two clear actions.
- Sections alternate quiet light surfaces with focused black product stages.
- Blue is reserved for action, selection, and system status.
